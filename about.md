This site is a personal technical notebook dedicated to research notes, system design write-ups, and engineering experiments.

Key areas of interest include:

* **AI Agents & Multi-Agent Orchestration**: Designing robust agent architectures, bounded execution contexts, and reliable reasoning pipelines.
* **Reasoning Systems & World Models**: Grounding generative language models in structured domain representations and deterministic reality.
* **Agent Governance & Safety**: Separating probabilistic model outputs from institutional authority, with deterministic verification and explicit human decision gates.
* **Financial AI & Quantitative Risk**: Applying autonomous and governed agent workflows to institutional risk investigation, credit events, and financial analysis.

All notes and articles published here reflect personal research and engineering experiments.
